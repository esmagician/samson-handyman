Questions

Service-area questions

Does Samson Handyman cover my postcode?

We cover Reading, including Calcot, Twyford, Caversham, Tilehurst, Woodley, Earley, Winnersh, Wargrave, Sonning, Henley-on-Thames, Wokingham, Bracknell, Maidenhead, Windsor, Slough and Ascot. Send your postcode to check our schedule. High Wycombe and other areas are by arrangement.

Is travel included?

Yes. Travel is included in our listed service area, with no extra travel charge. For work outside that area, I’ll agree any travel cost with you before booking.

Can I book several jobs together?

Yes. Send the whole list, with a photo of each job if possible, so I can plan the time and arrange the right team member.

Do you cover rental and commercial properties?

Yes. We work with homeowners, landlords, letting and estate agencies, and local businesses. For a rental repair list, see our property maintenance service.
