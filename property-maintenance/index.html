Clear scope

Practical maintenance, not specialist certification

This service is for suitable practical maintenance work. It is not a legal compliance inspection or certification service. Gas and electrical certification, fire-risk assessments and specialist fire-door work need appropriately qualified specialists. If a job needs a dedicated qualified trade, that can be identified before work starts.
