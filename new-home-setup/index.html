Important scope

Practical setup work, not a professional survey or compliance service

New-home setup is for visible, practical tasks and small repairs. It is not a professional snagging survey, a building inspection or a compliance certification service. Samson does not issue reports, certificates or guarantees about the condition of a new build or property.

Gas, electrical certification, structural concerns, major plumbing, fire-safety work and other regulated or specialist issues should be assessed by the appropriate qualified professional.

Before you book

Questions about move-in setup help

Can I send one list for several rooms?

Yes. A room-by-room list with photos and item links is the best way to see what can be grouped into a visit.

Can supplied furniture be assembled?

Yes. Send the product links, room details and any access restrictions before the visit is agreed.

Can you deal with a new-build snag list?

Samson can consider practical visible items, but this is not a formal snagging survey, inspection or certification service.

Can I send photos on WhatsApp?

Yes. Photos, a short room list and the preferred date help Samson check the job and reply more clearly.
