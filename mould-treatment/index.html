Service overview

Treat the visible surface after dealing with the moisture source

Painting over mould without addressing why the area is damp rarely lasts. The first step is to understand whether the marks follow condensation, poor ventilation or a previous leak and confirm that the surface is now dry.

Where the affected area is small, accessible and suitable for handyman treatment, it can be cleaned, treated, prepared and redecorated as one clearly scoped job.

Suitable treatment

What a small surface-mould visit can include

Clean and treat

Suitable visible growth is cleaned from the accessible surface and treated with an appropriate fungicidal product following its instructions.

Prepare and stain block

Dry, sound surfaces can be prepared and sealed where residual marking could otherwise show through the new finish.

Repaint the area

The repaired section can be finished with suitable paint, including mould-resistant paint where appropriate for the room and surface.

Clear scope

When a specialist is the better choice

This is not a damp survey, air-quality test or whole-building mould-remediation service. Hidden, widespread, recurring or heavily contaminated areas need specialist assessment.

Samson will not seal over an active leak or wet substrate. If the likely source is uncertain, the area is extensive, or occupants have health concerns, arrange a qualified damp, plumbing or mould-remediation specialist first.

Usually suitable

  • Small accessible wall or ceiling areas
  • Dry surfaces after a resolved leak
  • Localised condensation marking
  • Decorative repair after treatment

Needs specialist advice

  • Large or multiple affected rooms
  • Hidden mould or structural damp
  • Active leaks or wet plaster
  • Testing, remediation or health-risk concerns

Before you book

Questions about small-area mould treatment

Can mould be treated before a leak is fixed?

No. An active leak or other moisture source must be resolved first or the mould is likely to return.

Is this whole-property remediation?

No. It is for suitable small, accessible areas. Widespread, hidden or recurring mould needs a damp or mould-remediation specialist.

Can the area be repainted?

Yes, where it is dry and sound. Suitable surfaces can be prepared, stain blocked and repainted with an appropriate finish.

What photos should I send?

Send a close-up, a wider room view and the approximate size, plus any details about condensation, ventilation or a previous leak.
