About Samson Handyman

I’m Edvardas. I manage a small team of handymen, plumbers, painters and decorators, and carpenters. We help homeowners, landlords, letting and estate agencies, and local businesses with repairs, maintenance, decorating and home improvements. We’re based in Twyford and work across Reading and the surrounding areas.

What Samson does

Speak to me and I’ll arrange the right person for the job

I’m your point of contact, from the first message to arranging the visit. Whether it’s a repair at home, a room that needs decorating or a maintenance list for a rental property, send me the details and we’ll work out what’s needed.

Quotes are discussed clearly before work starts. Photos, measurements, location and access details help confirm whether the job is suitable and make the estimate more accurate.

Where regulated or specialist work is required, the job is assessed first so a dedicated qualified trade specialist can be used instead.
