Questions

Outdoor repair questions

Can you repair a loose fence panel or garden gate?

Small panel, rail, hinge, latch and gate-alignment repairs can be assessed from clear photos and a short explanation of what is loose, damaged or catching.

Can I send photos before booking?

Yes. A wide photo of the area, a closer photo of the problem and rough measurements are the quickest way to check a small outdoor repair and provide a clearer estimate.

What outdoor work is not suitable for this service?

This service is for smaller planned exterior repairs and adjustments. Major structural rebuilds, elevated specialist work, large storm damage and landscaping projects need the relevant specialist contractor.

Can several outdoor jobs be grouped together?

Yes. A gate, fence, decking and other small exterior repair-list items at the same property can often be reviewed together for a more efficient visit.
