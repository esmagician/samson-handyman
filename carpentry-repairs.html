Questions

Door and carpentry repair questions

Can you repair a door that sticks or rubs?

Many internal-door issues can be assessed from photos and details of where it catches. Hinges, clearances, handles and the visible frame condition are checked before the repair is agreed.

Can I send photos before booking?

Yes. A photo of the hinges, handle, frame and affected edge, plus your area and a short description, is the quickest way to check the job and provide a clearer estimate.

Do you work on fire doors or specialist security doors?

No. Fire doors, complex uPVC mechanisms and security-critical entrance work need the appropriate specialist and are not treated as ordinary handyman repairs.

Can several small repairs be grouped together?

Yes. Door, skirting, trim, shelf, handle and other practical repair-list jobs can often be reviewed together when they are in the same property.
