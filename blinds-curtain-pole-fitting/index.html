Service overview

One tidy visit for the finishing jobs around your windows

New blinds and curtains can look simple until the wall, lintel, brackets and fixing points need checking. This practical fitting service is for supplied items that need careful positioning, level fitting and the right fixings for the surface.

It works well for homeowners, rental properties and rooms being set up after a move. If there are several windows or nearby small jobs, include them in one list so the visit can be planned properly.

What can be fitted

Common curtain and blind fitting jobs

Curtain poles and tracks

Standard curtain poles, bay-window sections, curtain rails and tracks can be fitted where the wall condition and supplied fixings are suitable for the job.

Ready-to-fit blinds

Roller, Roman and Venetian blind fittings can be positioned carefully and checked for level operation once the supplied product is ready to install.

Grouped finishing jobs

Curtain and blind fitting can be combined with mirrors, shelves, handles or other small setup jobs in the same property where practical.

Clear scope

What to arrange before booking

Please have the blinds, poles, tracks and brackets available at the property, or send the product link first. Measurements, wall type and access can then be discussed before confirming the job.

This is a fitting service, not blind manufacture, a specialist window-repair service or electrical work on powered blinds. If a job needs specialist advice or non-standard structural work, Samson will explain that before work is agreed.

Before you book

Questions about curtain and blind fitting

Can I send photos first?

Yes. WhatsApp photos of the window, wall and supplied item are often the quickest way to start a quote.

Do I need to buy the items first?

Usually yes. Send the product link or instructions before booking so the fitting can be reviewed clearly.

Can several windows be done together?

Yes. Include the number of windows and a photo of each type so the visit can be planned sensibly.

Can this be combined with other jobs?

Often, yes. Mention mirrors, shelves, handles or other small finishing work in the same enquiry.
