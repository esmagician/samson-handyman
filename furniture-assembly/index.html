Questions

Questions about furniture assembly

Can you assemble several items in one visit?

Yes. Send me the full list and product links so I can estimate the work together and allow enough time.

Can you fix wardrobes and tall units to the wall?

Where the product instructions call for it and the wall is suitable, we can include the fixing in the agreed job. Send a photo of the wall and mention any restrictions before booking.

Do I need to buy the furniture first?

Send me the product link before or after you buy it. We can discuss the space and access first, then arrange a visit once delivery is confirmed.

How much does furniture assembly cost?

Small handyman jobs start at £60 for up to one hour, including materials and travel in our service area. Larger wardrobes and several items are quoted separately from the product details. I’m not VAT registered, so no VAT is added.
