Service overview

Refresh the worn parts while keeping sound finishes in place

Samson assesses the visible condition from photos first, agrees a clear scope and handles suitable accessible repairs with careful preparation and tidy finishing.

Clear boundaries

Visible repair work, not hidden leak diagnosis

A sound, dry surface is essential before joints or decorative finishes are renewed. If the problem points to failed waterproofing, hidden damage or active moisture, the underlying issue needs specialist attention first.

Needs a specialist

  • Active or hidden leaks
  • Failed tanking or waterproofing

Before you book

Bathroom repair questions

Can old silicone be removed and replaced?

Yes, where the joint and surrounding surfaces are accessible, dry and sound. The old material is removed before the joint is prepared and resealed.

Can only part of the grout be repaired?

Sometimes. Localised repair is suitable when the tiles remain secure and the affected section can be prepared properly. Widespread failure may need a larger scope.

Can one cracked tile be replaced?

It may be possible when a matching replacement is available and removing the damaged tile will not compromise the surrounding area.

What photos should I send?

Send a wide bathroom view, close-ups of each joint or damaged area, the postcode and any information about leaks or movement.
