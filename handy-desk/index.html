Handy Desk Email

Handy Desk Email is Edvardas Samsonas’s private email integration for Samson Handyman. It helps the owner review customer enquiries, agency instructions and work updates, and check whether a business conversation needs a reply.

What the integration does

With the owner’s Google authorisation, it reads original incoming and sent email. Recognised handyman messages can be saved in the owner’s private Handy Desk inbox. Scheduled checks run on the business’s cloud server and report incomplete access or history honestly.

The Google permission for this connection is read-only. It cannot send email, create drafts, change labels, mark messages read or delete mail. Invoice and customer-message actions use separate connections and owner approvals.

Optional confirmed booking sync

With separate Google authorisation, the owner can mirror explicitly confirmed Handy Desk appointments into a dedicated Google Tasks list and an app-created Google calendar. Only the saved business job title, address, appointment time and private job link are mirrored. Changed bookings require reconfirmation. Completing a Google task does not change job completion, invoices or payments in Handy Desk.

Who can use it

This is an owner-operated integration for Samson Handyman, not a public account-registration service. The business workspace requires the owner’s authenticated access. This public page describes the integration and contains no inbox or job information.
