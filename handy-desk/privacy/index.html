Handy Desk Email

Email integration privacy policy

Last updated: 12 October 2026

Operator and purpose

Edvardas Samsonas operates Handy Desk Email for Samson Handyman. It is used for private business administration: identifying handyman enquiries and work updates, keeping business records, and checking outstanding replies. Contact samsonashandyman@gmail.com about this integration or your information.

Google data accessed

After the mailbox owner grants access, the integration can read the Gmail account identity, message and thread identifiers, labels, sender and recipient headers, dates, subjects, incoming and sent message content, and attachment metadata. Original worker or supplier invoice PDFs may be retrieved only when authorised for the private invoice archive. The Gmail connection uses the read-only permission and cannot send, edit or delete mail.

How the data is used and stored

Original messages are reviewed to recognise genuine handyman business requests and to check later replies. Recognised business text, source identity and relevant dates may be saved in the owner’s private Handy Desk. Uncertain evidence is kept as a review gap rather than treated as a confirmed job or payment. Unrelated personal message bodies are not deliberately archived by this workflow. Raw emails and general email attachments are not copied into the business archive.

Authorised invoice originals and necessary business records are retained for business administration and applicable record-keeping obligations. Connection credentials and service backups are stored in private access-controlled locations. Disconnecting Google access stops future Gmail reads but does not automatically remove previously saved business records or backups.

Service providers and sharing

Google supplies Gmail and account authorisation. The owner’s connected OpenAI assistant may process message evidence to identify business requests and replies. Oracle hosts the private automation runtime, and Cloudflare and ChatGPT Sites support the owner’s private Handy Desk storage and application. Data is processed through these services only to support the integration’s business functions. Gmail data is not sold or used by Handy Desk Email for advertising, and Handy Desk Email does not use Gmail data to train general-purpose AI models.

Handy Desk Email’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. Human access to mailbox evidence is limited to the authorised owner, assistance requested by the owner, and situations allowed by those requirements.

Control, access and deletion

The Google account owner can revoke the connection from Google Account connections. You may contact Samson Handyman to request access, correction or deletion of relevant business information. Some records may need to remain for legal or business record-keeping obligations. Removing saved information from active storage does not instantly remove historical private backups.

This integration’s Google-data practices are separate from the public website’s optional advertising and analytics. See the Samson Handyman website privacy policy for website enquiries and measurement.
