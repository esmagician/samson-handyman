Handy Desk Email

Terms of use

Last updated: 12 October 2026

Handy Desk Email is a private business-administration integration operated by Edvardas Samsonas for Samson Handyman. It is not offered as a public email service or a public customer portal.

Authorised access

The integration is used only with the account owner’s authorisation. The owner is responsible for reviewing business evidence, keeping access private and using the integration lawfully. Google access can be withdrawn through the Google account’s connection controls.

Review and service limits

Automated identification can be incomplete or mistaken. Saved enquiries, outstanding-reply checks and invoice evidence require owner review. A message, job status or automated summary does not by itself prove an agreed price, work completion, delivery or payment. Financial actions and customer communications follow their separate owner-approval workflows.

Gmail reading depends on Google authorisation, provider availability and the available source history. The connection is read-only. These terms do not change the terms of Google or any other service provider, or the terms for actual handyman work.

Optional booking sync is separately authorised. Only an explicit owner confirmation on the exact saved appointment allows it into the dedicated Google task list and calendar. Booking changes require reconfirmation. An arrival marker without an agreed end time does not estimate work duration. A task tick or calendar entry is not an invoice, payment, worker-attendance confirmation or proof that work was completed.

Privacy and contact

Read the Handy Desk Email privacy policy for the integration’s data practices. Contact samsonashandyman@gmail.com with questions.
