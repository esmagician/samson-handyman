Common jobs

What you can ask us to help with

TV bracket fitting

Fixed, tilting and swivel brackets fitted where the bracket, TV and wall are compatible. We agree the position with you before drilling.

Wall type and fixings

Brick, block and plasterboard walls need different approaches. We check the fixing points and support available before deciding how to mount the TV.

A tidy finish

Level placement and an agreed plan for visible cables. Tell me if you want other shelves, mirrors or small fitting jobs included in the same visit.

Planning the fitting

The wall and bracket matter as much as the screen

Tell me the TV model and send a photo of the wall, including nearby sockets. We’ll discuss the height and position, check bracket compatibility and agree the cable finish before work starts.

Surface cable trunking can be discussed as part of the quote. Concealing cables inside a wall or moving sockets needs separate assessment.

For shelves, pictures or curtain fittings at the same visit, see our handyman services and blinds and curtain poles.

Already bought a bracket?

Send the model or a link, along with your TV size. I’ll check what is needed before arranging the visit. If you haven’t bought one yet, we can discuss a suitable option first.

Questions

Questions about tv wall mounting

Can you mount a TV on plasterboard?

It depends on the wall construction, available support, TV weight and bracket. Send photos and the TV and bracket models so we can assess it before confirming the fitting.

Is a TV bracket included?

The bracket and any cable-covering work are agreed in the quote. Let me know whether you already have a bracket or need help choosing one.

Can you hide the cables?

We can discuss a tidy surface route or trunking. Work inside walls and any socket changes need a separate assessment and are only included if agreed.

What does TV mounting cost?

Small handyman jobs start at £60 for up to one hour, including materials and travel within our service area. The TV, bracket, wall and cable requirements determine the quote for your job. No VAT is added.
